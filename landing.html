How DataLens Works

Transform raw CSV files into structured analytical intelligence in three straightforward steps.

1

Upload Your CSV File

Provide any standard CSV file regardless of row count or column schema. DataLens parses headers, separates numeric and categorical fields, and establishes your dataset as the single source of truth.

2

Automated Profiling & Quality Audit

Algorithms calculate missing cell counts, identify duplicate records, compute completeness percentages, and derive full descriptive statistics including mean, median, standard deviation, and quartiles.

3

Explore, Visualize & Export

Navigate your data with search and column filters, review real previews with responsive pagination, construct Chart.js visualizations, and export comprehensive dataset quality audit reports.

Capabilities

Engineered for Data Accuracy & Exploration

Everything required for dataset quality auditing, statistical distributions, and exploratory data analysis.

Dynamic CSV Parsing

Calculations are computed dynamically from your user-uploaded file. Never capped by hardcoded limits or synthetic sample defaults.

Comprehensive Data Quality Audit

Audits missing values per column, detects exact duplicates, verifies record completeness, and computes an overall health score.

Descriptive Statistics

Computes min, max, mean, median, variance, standard deviation, and 25th/75th percentiles for numerical columns, plus categorical frequencies.

Interactive Data Explorer

Quickly filter, sort, and search across all dataset columns with paginated previews rendering real records directly from your CSV.

Chart.js Visualizations

Create real-time bar charts, distribution curves, trend lines, and doughnut category breakdowns powered exclusively by your actual columns.

Rule-Based Quality Insights

Deterministic, rule-based alerts and hygiene recommendations based on your dataset metrics without third-party AI dependencies.

About DataLens

Built for students, analysts, and engineers who demand deterministic, reproducible dataset profiling.

Data Profiling Without Black Boxes

DataLens was created to provide a reliable, transparent environment for evaluating dataset health before data enters downstream analysis, reporting, or machine learning pipelines.

Instead of opaque automated heuristics, DataLens employs established mathematical formulas for statistical dispersion and deterministic data quality auditing. Your data remains completely yours.

The platform is built on proven web standards—HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, Java Servlets, and JDBC persistence—ensuring optimal speed, stability, and reliability.

User-Uploaded Source of Truth
Every metric, preview row, chart point, and insight is computed directly from your uploaded CSV.
Strict Privacy & User Isolation
Each user's active datasets and sessions are strictly isolated. User A can never access User B's datasets.
Zero Black-Box Dependencies
No external AI APIs or third-party cloud data brokers. Deterministic rules ensure 100% reproducible results.
